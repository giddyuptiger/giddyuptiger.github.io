How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Create your account, connect Claude once, and let it take over the upkeep.

1

Create your Maestro account

Sign up with an email and password. That creates your own private space — an account isolated from everyone else's, where every row is visible only to you.

Create your account arrow_forward
mail You'll get a confirmation email — click the link to verify, then you're signed in. Use this same account when you connect Claude in step 2.
2

Add the Maestro connector in Claude

This is the link that lets Claude read and update your Maestro. In Claude, open Settings and add Maestro as a custom connector:

Settingschevron_right Connectorschevron_right Add custom connector

Paste this URL, then click Add:

https://cixmyhdzzbgbltmitzkb.supabase.co/functions/v1/maestro-connector
lock Claude will open a sign-in screen — sign in with the same Maestro account from step 1. That securely links Claude to your space over OAuth. Approve the one-time prompt and you're connected.
3

Let Claude be your chief of staff

Now just work with Claude the way you already do. Ask it to run a project, track a goal, or capture a note — and it reads and writes your Maestro as it goes. Open your view any time to see where everything stands and clear what needs you.
